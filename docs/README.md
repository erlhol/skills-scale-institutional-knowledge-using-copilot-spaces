# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

Welcome to the central hub for OctoAcme's project management process docs. These guides standardize how we run projects, from initiation through planning, execution, release, and continuous improvement.

## Summary of OctoAcme Project Management Processes

OctoAcme's project management framework is built on five core phases—Initiation, Planning, Execution, Release, and Retrospective—with a focus on customer-first delivery and iterative development. The approach emphasizes clear roles (Project Manager, Product Manager, Developers, QA, and Stakeholders), structured artifacts (one-pagers, roadmaps, risk registers), and transparent communication. Risk management is integrated throughout the project lifecycle, with Quality Assurance embedded from planning through deployment. By standardizing processes and capturing lessons learned in retrospectives, OctoAcme transforms individual project insights into organizational knowledge, reducing dependency risk and accelerating onboarding while maintaining consistent, repeatable execution.

## Key Characteristics

✅ **Customer-First & Iterative** — Delivery focused on customer value with regular feedback loops  
✅ **Role Clarity** — Defined personas and responsibilities eliminate ambiguity  
✅ **Risk Management** — Proactive identification and mitigation throughout the lifecycle  
✅ **Quality Embedded** — QA involvement from planning through deployment

✅ **Scalability** — Consistent approach that scales across multiple concurrent initiatives
